Sinigang

Description

Servings Preparation Time Cooking Time
4 people 15 minutes 1 hour

Sinigang is a Filipino sour soup made with meat, vegetables, and a sour broth. It is a popular Filipino dish known for its delicious and refreshing sour taste.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
Cooking Oil 2-3 Tablespoon
Pork 1 kg
Water 9 cups
Tomatoes 2 pieces
Onion 1 piece
Radish 1 piece
Kangkong 1 bunch
Sitaw 6-7 peices
Okra 5 peices
Talong 1 peice
Gabi 1 peice
Green Chili 3 peices
Fish Sauce 2 & 1/2 Tablespoon
Black Pepper 1/4 Teaspoon
Salt 1/4 Teaspoon
Sinigang mix 44 grams

Steps

  1. Pour the cooking oil in the pot
  2. Then saute the onion and tomato
  3. Then add the pork and add the salt and pepper
  4. Then add the Gabi, Radish and mix
  5. Then pour 9 cups of water and brint it to boil
  6. Simmer for 30 minutes or until the pork is tender
  7. Then add the Green Chili, Sitaw, Okra, and Eggplant
  8. Cook for 3 minutes
  9. Then add the fish sauce and the sinigang mix
  10. And simmer for 3 minutes
  11. and lastly add the kangkong and cook for 1 minute
