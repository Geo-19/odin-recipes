Sinigang

Description

Sinigang is a Filipino sour soup made with meat, vegetables, and a sour broth. It is a popular Filipino dish known for its delicious and refreshing sour taste.

Ingredients

  • Pork
  • Tamarind soup mix
  • Water
  • Tomatoes
  • Onion
  • Radish
  • String beans
  • Water spinach (kangkong)
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Boil the pork in a pot of water until tender.
  2. Add the tomatoes and onion.
  3. Add the tamarind soup mix and stir.
  4. Add the radish and string beans.
  5. Cook until the vegetables are tender.
  6. Add the kangkong and cook for a few more minutes.
  7. Season with salt to taste.
  8. Serve hot with rice.
