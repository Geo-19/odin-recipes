Sisig

Servings Preparation Time Cooking Time
4 people 20 minutes 45 minutes

Description

Sisig is a popular Filipino dish made with chopped pork, onions, and seasonings. It is known for its savory, crispy, and flavorful taste.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
Pork 500 g
Onion 2 pieces
Garlic 3 cloves
Chili 2 pieces
Mayonnaise 2 tablespoons
Calamansi juice 2 tablespoons
Salt and pepper To taste

Steps

  1. Cook the pork until tender.
  2. Chop the pork into small pieces.
  3. Heat a pan and cook the chopped pork until crispy.
  4. Add the garlic and onion.
  5. Mix and cook until the onion becomes soft.
  6. Add calamansi juice, salt, pepper, and chili peppers.
  7. Add mayonnaise and mix well.
  8. Serve hot.
