Sisig

Description

Servings Preparation Time Cooking Time
4 people 20 minutes 45 minutes

Sisig is a popular Filipino dish made with chopped pork, onions, and seasonings. It is known for its savory, crispy, and flavorful taste.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
Pork Belly 1 & 1/2 lbss
Chopped Onions 2 pieces
Thai Chili Pepper 4 peices
Long Green Pepper 2 pieces
Mayonnaise 1/4 tablespoons
Knorr SavorRich Pork 1 sachet
Ground blackpepper 1/2 teaspoon
Margarine 1/4 cup

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a deep cooking pot or deep fryer. Fry the boiled pork belly until golden brown and crispy.
  2. chop the crispy pork belly into small pieces.
  3. Heat half the margarine in a pan and saute half the onion until soft.
  4. Add chopped pork and saute for 30 seconds.
  5. Add knorr SavorRich, Stir and the nadd chille pepers and season with ground black pepper.
  6. Cook for 1 minute.
  7. Add the mayonaisse, Stir and add the remaining onion and set it aside.
  8. Heat a metal plate (sizzling plate) on a stovetop and melt the remaining margarine.
  9. Transfer the crispy pork sisig into the metal plate.
  10. Let it star for 30 seconds.
  11. And lastly serve with warm rice and your favorite beverage>
